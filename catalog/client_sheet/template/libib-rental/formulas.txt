LITTLE MOVIE STORE — Libib rental upload sheet: the two formulas

Tab 1 is `Add rentals` (the client types here), tab 2 is `Libib import` (the
download). Plus the hidden `mappings` tab: import ../genre-mappings.csv
(16 rows, genre name in A, handle in B), exactly as for the floor sale sheet.

Tab 1 layout:
  A Title   B Format   C Genre 1   D Genre 2   E Genre 3   F Copies
  G Description        H Extra tags
  I Check   (formula 1 below — nothing is typed in I)
  K1        status line (below)

Python mirror: catalog/client_sheet/libib_rental.py. Answer key for the
scaffold rows: libib-rental-template.expected.csv. Guide: libib-rental-guide.md.

------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMULA 1 — PASTE INTO `Add rentals`, CELL I1:
------------------------------------------------------------------

=VSTACK("Check", BYROW(A2:H, LAMBDA(r, LET(
  title,   TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 1))),
  fmt,     TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 2))),
  genA,    TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 3))),
  genB,    TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 4))),
  genC,    TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 5))),
  copies,  TRIM(TO_TEXT(INDEX(r, 1, 6))),
  unknown, LAMBDA(g, IF(g = "", "", IF(ISNA(MATCH(g, mappings!$A:$A, 0)), "unknown genre '" & g & "'", ""))),
  probs,   TEXTJOIN("; ", TRUE,
             IF(title = "", "no title", ""),
             IF(ISNA(MATCH(fmt, {"VHS";"DVD";"Blu-Ray";"4K";"Laserdisc";"Betamax"}, 0)), "pick a format", ""),
             IF(genA & genB & genC = "", "pick a genre", ""),
             unknown(genA), unknown(genB), unknown(genC),
             IF(copies = "", "", IF(REGEXMATCH(copies, "^[1-9]\d*$"), "", "copies must be a whole number"))),
  IF(TEXTJOIN("", TRUE, r) = "", "", IF(probs = "", "OK", probs))
))))

------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS LINE — PASTE INTO `Add rentals`, CELL K1:
------------------------------------------------------------------

="Ready to import: " & COUNTIF(I2:I, "OK") & "   ·   Held back: " & (COUNTIF(I2:I, "?*") - COUNTIF(I2:I, "OK"))

------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMULA 2 — PASTE INTO `Libib import`, CELL A1:
------------------------------------------------------------------

=LET(
  raw,     IFERROR(FILTER('Add rentals'!A2:H, 'Add rentals'!I2:I = "OK"), ""),
  empty,   COLUMNS(raw) = 1,
  src,     IF(empty, {"","","","","","","",""}, raw),
  ws,      LAMBDA(t, TRIM(REGEXREPLACE(TO_TEXT(t), "[\s\x{00A0}]+", " "))),
  slug,    LAMBDA(t, REGEXREPLACE(REGEXREPLACE(REGEXREPLACE(LOWER(TRIM(TO_TEXT(t))), "['’]", ""), "[^a-z0-9]+", "-"), "^-+|-+$", "")),
  gh,      LAMBDA(g, IF(TRIM(g) = "", "", IFERROR(VLOOKUP(TRIM(g), mappings!$A:$B, 2, FALSE), ""))),
  title,   MAP(INDEX(src,,1), LAMBDA(t, ws(t))),
  tags,    MAP(INDEX(src,,2), INDEX(src,,3), INDEX(src,,4), INDEX(src,,5), INDEX(src,,8),
             LAMBDA(f, a, b, c, e, TEXTJOIN(", ", TRUE, UNIQUE(HSTACK(slug(f), gh(a), gh(b), gh(c),
               IF(TRIM(e) = "", "", ARRAYFORMULA(LOWER(TRIM(SPLIT(e, ",")))))), TRUE)))),
  copies,  MAP(INDEX(src,,6), LAMBDA(c, IF(TRIM(TO_TEXT(c)) = "", "1", TRIM(TO_TEXT(c))))),
  descr,   MAP(INDEX(src,,7), LAMBDA(d, ws(d))),
  konst,   LAMBDA(v, MAP(title, LAMBDA(x, v))),
  blank,   konst(""),
  header,  {"title","creators","description","upc_isbn10","ean_isbn13","number_of_discs","ensemble","aspect_ratio","tags","notes","group","price","added","publisher","publish_date","length_of","copies","call_number","ddc","lcc","rating","review","review_created","status","began_date","completed_date"},
  body,    HSTACK(title, blank, descr, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, tags, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, copies, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank, blank),
  IF(empty, header, VSTACK(header, body))
)

------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES FOR WHOEVER BUILDS THE SHEET
------------------------------------------------------------------

* Only rows whose Check reads OK reach `Libib import`. A held-back row is
  fixed on `Add rentals` and appears in the download by itself — nothing to
  re-run.

* The 26 output columns and their order are Libib's Movie import template
  (catalog/libib/columns.py:LIBIB_MOVIE_COLUMNS). Do not add, remove or
  reorder them.

* call_number is blank on purpose. The rental sync app only picks up copies
  with a `201…` barcode and no Call # (catalog/core/barcodes.py), and Libib
  gives every new copy its own `201…` barcode. price is blank too: nothing
  downstream reads it.

* No UPC/EAN: the import never asks Libib to look a movie up, so title,
  description and tags are exactly what the client typed. Force Import Mode
  is what lets a row in without one. Posters come from TMDB on the sync page.

* Tags: the format as a slug (`blu-ray`, `4k`), the genre handles from
  `mappings` (`kids-family`), then each extra tag — all lowercase, repeats
  dropped. That is the vocabulary rental_sync/transform.py checks, so a
  ready row can't be set aside by the sync app for its format or genre.
  (An extra tag that looks like a misspelt genre, e.g. `horor`, still is.)

* Copies: blank means 1. Libib makes that many copies of the one item, each
  with its own barcode — and the sync app makes each its own Shopify product.

* Same Sheets rules as the other upload sheets: TRIM isn't array-aware
  outside ARRAYFORMULA, so it's mapped per cell; LET names must not look like
  cell references (`genA`, never `g1`); `konst` repeats a constant down the
  rows; SPLIT errors on an empty string, hence the IF around it. UNIQUE(…,
  TRUE) dedupes across the row.

* Seam test: sheet-export-input.csv (`Add rentals`, with Check) and
  sheet-export.csv (`Libib import`) are downloads of the live sheet, checked
  against the Python mirror in tests/catalog/test_libib_rental_sheet.py.
  After any formula change, re-download both tabs over those two files.
